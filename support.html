How to play

  • Touch anywhere, pull back like a slingshot and let go to shoot.
  • Arrows pierce: hit several birds with one arrow for a combo.
  • You have three hearts. A bird that escapes, a dove you hit, or an egg landing on your head costs one.
  • Golden birds give points, arrows and a heart back.
  • Finish a level with hearts left to win stars, and use stars to unlock new archers.

Questions

Where is my progress saved?
On your device, and in your iCloud account if iCloud is on. With iCloud, your stars and archers follow you to your other iPhone or iPad.

The leaderboard doesn’t show my score.
Make sure you are signed in to Game Center in your device’s Settings. There are leaderboards for Endless, for the Daily Challenge and for your total stars.

Can I play upright, or left-handed?
Yes. Open the settings (the gear on the main menu) to switch on portrait play or move the archer to the other side.

There’s no sound.
Check Music and Sound in the settings (the gear on the main menu).

How do I stop the daily reminder?
Turn off Reminder in the settings.
