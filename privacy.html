Privacy Policy

Effective 25 October 2026

Fletch: Bird Archery (“the game”) is a free game with no ads. It is built so that we never collect, store or share any personal information about you.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it contains no third-party code that collects data.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a few things on your own device only: the stars you have won on each level, your best scores, the archer you picked, and your sound, music and vibration settings. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Deleting the game deletes it.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple’s Game Center, the game can submit your Endless best score to its Game Center leaderboard. Game Center is a service provided by Apple; Apple handles that information under its own privacy policy, and we do not receive any personal information from it. You can sign out of Game Center at any time in your device’s Settings.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the way the game handles information ever changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change with it.
