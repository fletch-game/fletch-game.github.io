Privacy Policy

Effective 25 October 2026

Fletch: Bird Archery (“the game”) is a free game with no ads. It is built so that we never collect, store or share any personal information about you.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it contains no third-party code that collects data.

Your progress

To remember your progress, the game saves a few things on your device: the stars you have won on each level, your best scores, the archers and arrow trails you have won, and your settings.

If you use iCloud, the same progress is also kept in your own iCloud account, so it carries over to your other devices. This is Apple’s iCloud service; we cannot see or read it, and nothing is sent to us.

The home screen widget only reads the day’s challenge and your streak from the game on the same device.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple’s Game Center, the game sends your scores, achievements and challenge results to Game Center so they can appear on its leaderboards and to your friends. Game Center is a service provided by Apple; Apple handles that information under its own privacy policy, and we do not receive any personal information from it. You can sign out of Game Center at any time in your device’s Settings.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the game can remind you about the daily challenge. These reminders are scheduled on your device; no server is involved. You can turn them off in the game’s settings.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the way the game handles information ever changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change with it.
